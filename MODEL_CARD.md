# Model card – TrialScout trial termination risk

Generated by `src/model/card.py` from `models/model_metadata.json`, `reports/model_validation.json`, `data/LEAKAGE_AUDIT.json` and (once run) `reports/model_final_test.json`. Do not edit by hand.

## What it does

Given what is known about an interventional clinical trial **when it starts** (design, sponsor type, eligibility, disease area...), it estimates the probability that the trial ends as TERMINATED rather than COMPLETED, and turns it into a Low / Medium / High risk band.

**Intended use:** a portfolio and learning project; showing patterns in public registry data next to search results. **Not for:** decisions about real trials, patients, funding or investments.

> Research and portfolio tool. The risk score describes patterns in past registry data; it is not medical, legal or investment advice and does not judge any specific trial.

## Data

- Source: ClinicalTrials.gov API v2, interventional trials that started 2010–2020 and ended COMPLETED (label 0) or TERMINATED (label 1). WITHDRAWN trials (never enrolled anyone) and trials with no final outcome are not included.
- 25035 trials registered only after they ended were dropped (1.9% of them terminated): no prediction at start was ever possible for them.
- 1285 trials terminated because of COVID-19 (keyword match on `why_stopped`, used only for this flag). Data version used for this model: **`main`** (`main` leaves those trials out; `with_covid` keeps them).
- Split by start year: trained on 2010–2016 (77902 trials), tuned and calibrated on 2017, tested once on 2018; 2019–2020 reported separately.

## Features

58 features: 50 known at trial start, plus 8 *caution* features.

**Caution features** (planned at start, but the registry keeps only the latest version, so they may have been edited while the trial ran):

- `n_locations`
- `n_countries`
- `n_collaborators`
- `n_primary_outcomes`
- `n_secondary_outcomes`
- `eligibility_criteria_chars`
- `has_us_site`
- `multi_country`

Deliberately **not** used (see `data/LEAKAGE_AUDIT.md`): `why_stopped`, completion dates, results, `enrollment_count` (holds the *actual* final number; alone it would score AUC 0.741 on the training years, and live trials only have a planned number), sponsor names, start year, and the FDA-regulated fields (their meaning changed with a 2017 rule).

## Model

- `lightgbm` with settings {'min_child_samples': 200, 'n_estimators': 300, 'num_leaves': 31}, inside a scikit-learn pipeline (one-hot categories, median-filled and scaled numbers) fitted on the training years only.
- Calibration: `sigmoid`, fitted on 2017.
- Risk bands from the 2017 base rate b = 0.1207: Low p < 0.1207, Medium up to 0.2415, High above.
- Yes/no threshold for precision/recall: 0.1575 (best F1 on validation).
- File: `models/trial_risk_model.joblib`, 0.44 MB; scikit-learn 1.9.1, LightGBM 4.7.0.

## Limitations

- **Moderate predictive power.** Termination depends a lot on things not in the registry at the start (funding changes, recruitment problems, safety findings, business decisions). No single start-time feature separates the classes well: the best one alone reaches AUC 0.584 (`eligibility_criteria_chars`).
- **Latest record, not the start-time record.** The registry keeps only the current version of each trial, so even "known at start" fields may have been edited later.
- **Right-censoring.** Recent start years are missing their long-running trials, so 2019–2020 results are a stress test, not the main result.
- **Time and shocks.** Trained on 2010–2016 trials; practices and rules change (e.g. the 2017 rule change, COVID-19). Performance on future trials may differ.
- **Registry coverage.** Only trials registered on ClinicalTrials.gov (a US registry); trials listed only in other countries' registries are not included.
- **Association, not cause.** A feature linked to higher risk does not make a trial fail.
- **Label meaning.** TERMINATED covers very different reasons (safety, futility, funding, slow recruitment, business). The model does not say which.
